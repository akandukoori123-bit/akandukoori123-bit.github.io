I'm a student researcher in the **Algoverse AI Research Program**, working on
clinical LLM safety. My lead proposal was selected by the team for presentation
to the faculty PI.

The project tests whether clinical LLM agents retrieve missing patient
information before recommending contraindicated medications. It extends the
RxSafeBench benchmark (2,443 scenarios) into tool-augmented decision-making with
a ReAct-style agent harness, scoring tool use and recommendation safety
separately. Target venue: NeurIPS / ML4H workshop.
