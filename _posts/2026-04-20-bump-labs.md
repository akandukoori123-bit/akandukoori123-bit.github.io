I'm a co-founder of [**BUMP Labs**](https://bump-labs.com), building a wearable
atropine pump for crashing bradycardia. The device is patent pending (USPTO
provisional #63/686,176).

I co-developed the original concept and contributed to the device codebase. We
are now integrating the AUMP machine learning prediction layer with the BUMP
hardware to create a closed-loop predict-and-treat platform.
