I co-authored a controlled study of 30 elementary students comparing math skill
gains from interactive digital platforms against worksheet-based instruction.
Students using digital tools improved 24.2% on average versus 8.3% for the
control group.

I contributed to study design, assessment administration, and data analysis. The
paper is published as
[arXiv:2408.06689](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06689) (second author).
