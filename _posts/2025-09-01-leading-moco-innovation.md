I'm the President of **MoCo Innovation**, a STEM-education nonprofit working to put
real technology education in front of students who don't always get access to it.

A big part of the role is making the programs sustainable. I've helped raise
**$32,000+ in sponsorships** so we can expand across multiple schools — covering
hardware, materials, and instructor time instead of relying on whatever a single
classroom can scrape together.

The part I care about most is the teaching. I run coding and computational-thinking
lessons for elementary and K–12 students, both in person and online. Watching a
student go from "I could never do this" to debugging their own program is the
reason I keep showing up. Leading the organization has taught me as much about
logistics, fundraising, and people as it has about technology.
