**Falconwing** is a 3D-printed, Arduino-based prosthetic arm that grips using
your own muscle signals. MyoWare EMG sensors read muscle activity in real time,
and the Arduino translates it into a controlled grasping mechanism.

The hard part was making sensing, electronics, mechanics, and code cooperate
reliably: noisy EMG readings, drifting thresholds, and mechanical play all had
to be tuned together.
