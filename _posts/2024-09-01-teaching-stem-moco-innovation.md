I teach hands-on STEM classes to elementary students at Clarksburg Elementary
School through [**MoCo Innovation**](https://www.mocoinnovation.org), a nonprofit
serving underrepresented communities in Montgomery County.

I design the lesson curriculum and instructional materials used in the
classroom. Watching a student go from "I could never do this" to debugging their
own program is the reason I keep showing up.
