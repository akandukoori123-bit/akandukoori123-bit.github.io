**Biasmap** is a project for analyzing societal bias in large language models by
comparing what the models say against real-world sentiment.

The idea is simple to state and tricky to do well: take model outputs on a topic,
then line them up against sentiment drawn from news coverage and online discourse
about the same topic. Where the model consistently leans away from how the world
actually talks about something, that gap is a signal worth mapping.

Building it pushed me deeper into natural-language processing — collecting and
cleaning text data, scoring sentiment, and being careful about what a measured
"bias" does and doesn't prove. It's the kind of work where the analysis matters
as much as the result, and where it's easy to fool yourself if you're not rigorous.
