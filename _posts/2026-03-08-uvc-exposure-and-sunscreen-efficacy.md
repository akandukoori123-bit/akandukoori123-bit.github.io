title: "Testing sunscreen against UVC with living bacteria"
date: 2026-03-08 09:00:00 -0400

tags: [biology, experiment, microbiology]
---

This experiment asked a concrete question: does **SPF 30 sunscreen** actually
protect against **UVC** exposure, measured by whether bacteria survive it?

I used *Rhodospirillum rubrum* as the test organism and exposed samples to UVC,
comparing protected and unprotected conditions. By tracking survival across
exposure levels, I could estimate **lethal exposure thresholds** and see how much
difference the sunscreen made.

Everything went into a detailed research report — methods, results, and
conclusions — so the work could be evaluated and repeated. Designing a clean
experiment around a living organism made me a lot more careful about controls,
measurement, and not overstating what a single result can tell you.
