For this build I designed a **triangulation-based device** that measures angles,
distances, and the heights of objects you can't reach with a tape measure.

It combines a **clinometer** (for angle of elevation) with a **level** (to keep the
baseline honest). With a known distance and a measured angle, a little
trigonometry turns "I can't reach that" into a real number — the height of a tree,
a building, or anything else too tall to measure directly.

It's a small device, but it's a clean example of the thing I like most about
engineering: using basic math and a couple of simple instruments to measure
something that otherwise seems out of reach.
