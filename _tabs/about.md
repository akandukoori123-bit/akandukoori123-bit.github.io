I'm Aditya Kandukoori, a student in the Science, Math and Computer Science
magnet program at Poolesville High School.

I research clinical LLM safety with the Algoverse AI Research Program, where I
designed an agentic evaluation protocol for testing whether clinical LLM agents
retrieve missing patient information before recommending medications.

I built **AUMP**, a machine learning early-warning system that predicts crashing
bradycardia before standard monitors catch it. I'm also a co-founder of
[**BUMP Labs**](https://bump-labs.com), a patent-pending wearable closed-loop
cardiac response device that integrates AUMP's prediction layer.

I teach hands-on STEM classes to elementary students through
[**MoCo Innovation**](https://www.mocoinnovation.org), a nonprofit serving
underrepresented communities in Montgomery County. I also co-authored a study on
digital math tools published on arXiv.

Outside of that: taekwondo (black belt), swimming, table tennis, and art.
